The additional 50 IDs were already counted before the displayed cycle totals; they are not inferred additional research.

The bottleneck is demonstrably the early screen. Its individual rejection counts are not present in the uploaded transcript/decision file. Therefore the exact dominant predicate cannot be established. The old screen imposed a one-hour age limit, excluded all decorated listings and listed pattern-sensitive finishes, required an 8% discount against predicted price when present, and skipped missing references. Each could suppress discovery. The new version prints the breakdown to resolve this uncertainty.

The previous background requirements could also suppress future candidates: three independent asking sellers, five matching completed sales, cheapest-ask and lower-quartile valuation, the stable tier's 3% weekly limits, and strict stressed-profit floors. These were NOT the cause of the two actual uploaded rejections, but have been relaxed for the requested manual-review experiment.

The updated review model deliberately trades confidence for observable leads. Its sales median is a reference, not an executable sale offer. A reference-discount screenshot cannot establish independent fair value, sale timing, or future profitability. No historical replay of hypothetical successful alerts is possible from these two records and aggregate scan counters.
